My Top 4 Interior Design Styles

Harlem Deco/ Soul Train Revival

Harlem Deco, also known as Soul Train Revival, is a vibrant and eclectic design style that draws inspiration from the cultural movements of the 1970s. It features bold colors, geometric patterns, and a mix of vintage and contemporary furnishings. This style celebrates African American culture and the artistic expressions of the era. It's a way of expression through art, music, and fashion.

Mid-Century Modern

Mid-Century Modern is a design movement that emerged in the mid-20th century, characterized by clean lines, organic forms, and a seamless integration with nature. This style often features large windows, open spaces, and a minimalist aesthetic, reflecting the optimism and innovation of the post-war era. This style is all about simplicity and functionality, making it a popular choice for modern homes. Warmed wood tones and natural materials are often used to create a cozy and inviting atmosphere.

Traditional

Traditional design is characterized by classic details, rich colors, and a variety of textures. It often incorporates elements from various historical styles, creating a timeless and elegant aesthetic. Traditional interiors typically feature ornate furnishings, decorative moldings, and a warm color palette. Great care is taken to create a cohesive and harmonious look. Nice for entertaining and a traditional spaces often include formal dining rooms and comfortable living areas.

Bohemian

Bohemian design, often referred to as "boho," is a style that embraces a carefree, eclectic approach to decor. It is characterized by vibrant colors, mixed patterns, and an abundance of textiles. Bohemian interiors often feature a mix of vintage and global-inspired furnishings, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. Boho is comfortable and relaxed, making it perfect for those who want to express their individuality through their living spaces.
