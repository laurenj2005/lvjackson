Home Styles with Lauren Jackson

About the Website

Hi! I'm Lauren Jackson. I am a third year at the University of Georgia majoring in Cognitive Science with a minor in Educational Psychology and I am obtaining a New Media certificate. During COVID me and my mom would go to home open houses to get out the house. This is when I became obsessed with home design and decor. In my free time I watch HGTV, home/apartment hunting and look at zillow to find my future home. In this website I will show some of my favorite home styles interior and exterior!

Final Project Reflection

Overall this project was a learning experience for me. I was able to explore and create upon a previous project. Adding more interactive elements and improving the design were key focuses for me. I was able to use Html, CSS and JavaScript to enhance the user experience and make the website more engaging. The project I was able to create something that I am really proud of and I hope others enjoy it as well! During the duration of this project, I learned a lot about web development and design principles. I hope to further my knowledge and skills in this field and create more user-friendly experiences.
