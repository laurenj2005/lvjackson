Architectural Styles

My Top 4 Architectural Styles

Brownstone

Brownstone are iconic row houses characterized by their brown sandstone facades, typically found in urban areas. It has a rich history and is often associated with New York City.It is timeless and elegant. Brownstones often feature stoops, or front steps, that lead up to the entrance, adding to their charm. Ideal for those who appreciate classic architecture and urban living.

Cottage

Cottages are small, cozy homes, often found in rural or semi-rural areas. They are typically characterized by their quaint architecture, including steeply pitched roofs, exposed wooden beams, and charming gardens. Cottages evoke a sense of nostalgia and are often associated with a simpler, more peaceful way of life. This style are for those who appreciate charm and comfort in their living spaces and love green outdoor spaces.

Georgian

Georgian architecture is a style that was popular in the 18th and early 19th centuries. It is characterized by its symmetry, classic proportions, and decorative elements. Georgian homes often feature brick or stone facades, multi-paned windows, and elegant doorways. This style is classical and refined. It reflects the ideals of the Enlightenment and the growing middle class of the time. Best for those who want a touch of history and elegance in their living spaces.

Ranch

Ranch-style homes are single-story houses that emphasize open spaces and a casual lifestyle. They often feature large windows, sliding glass doors, and an integration with the surrounding landscape. This style became popular in the mid-20th century, particularly in suburban areas. Best for those who enjoy a laid-back lifestyle and easy access to outdoor spaces.
